# elmdb Corporate Contributor License Agreement

Version 1

This agreement lets a company or other organization contribute to elmdb through its employees or contractors. It
grants, for the Contributions its Designated Employees make, the same licenses as
[elmdb's Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) does, so the Maintainer can keep using, licensing and
distributing them. The Corporation keeps the copyright in its Contributions: this agreement grants licenses, it
doesn't transfer ownership.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Corporation"** means the legal entity that signs this agreement, together with every entity that controls it, is
  controlled by it, or is under common control with it. "Control" means the power, directly or indirectly, to direct
  an entity's management, by contract or otherwise, or ownership of fifty percent (50%) or more of its outstanding
  shares or beneficial ownership.
- **"Maintainer"** means Angel Santiago, the copyright holder of elmdb named in its license files, and any person or
  entity to whom the Maintainer transfers elmdb or the rights under this agreement.
- **"elmdb"** means the software in the repository at https://github.com/spankyed/elmdb and any work derived from it.
- **"Designated Employee"** means an employee or contractor of the Corporation listed in Schedule A, as amended under
  section 6.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including code, documentation and changes to existing works, that a
  Designated Employee intentionally submits to the Maintainer for inclusion in elmdb, by a pull request, a patch, an
  issue or any other communication sent to the Maintainer or elmdb's repository, that isn't conspicuously marked as
  "Not a Contribution", and in which the Corporation holds rights.

perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable license to reproduce, prepare derivative

The Corporation further grants to the Maintainer the right to license its Contributions, and works that include them,
under any license terms the Maintainer chooses, including licenses other than those elmdb is distributed under when
the Contribution is made, and including proprietary or commercial licenses.

## 3. What the Maintainer promises in return

Each Contribution the Maintainer distributes will remain available, in at least the version it first appears in,
under the license elmdb is distributed under when the Maintainer accepts it (at Version 1 of this agreement, MIT OR
Apache-2.0). Nothing in section 2 takes that away from anyone who received it.

## 5. The Corporation's representations

The Corporation represents that:

1. It is legally entitled to grant the licenses above, and the person signing for it is authorized to do so.
2. Each of its Contributions is an original creation of its Designated Employees. Where a Contribution includes work
   they didn't create, the Designated Employee says so when submitting it, identifies its source, and states any

3. It will tell the Maintainer if it learns of any fact or circumstance that would make these representations

## 6. Designated Employees

Schedule A lists the GitHub accounts of the Corporation's Designated Employees. The Corporation, through the person
who signed this agreement or another authorized representative, tells the Maintainer in writing when someone should
be added to or removed from Schedule A. A change takes effect when the Maintainer records it. Removing someone doesn't
revoke the licenses granted for the Contributions they made while listed.

Work a person contributes while listed is covered by this agreement. Work they contribute that the Corporation holds
no rights in, such as work on their own time, may need [elmdb's Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md)
instead.

## 7. No other obligations

The Corporation isn't expected to provide support for its Contributions, except to the extent it chooses to. Unless
required by applicable law or agreed to in writing, the Corporation provides its Contributions "AS IS", without
warranties or conditions of any kind, express or implied, including any warranties or conditions of title,
non-infringement, merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose. The Maintainer isn't obliged to use or include
any Contribution.

## 8. How to sign

A person authorized to sign for the Corporation opens an issue in elmdb's repository titled
"Corporate CLA: <the Corporation's name>". The issue holds no signature and no contact details: the Maintainer replies
there to arrange signing privately. The signed copy records:

- the Corporation's full legal name and address;
- the signatory's name, title and the date;
- Schedule A, the GitHub accounts of its Designated Employees.

Once the agreement is signed, the Maintainer adds the Corporation's name, the date it signed and its Designated
Employees' GitHub accounts to `.github/cla-corporate.json` in elmdb's repository. The pull requests of the accounts
listed there pass the contributor license agreement check without signing individually.

## Schedule A: Designated Employees

Kept with the signed copy, and by GitHub account in `.github/cla-corporate.json`.
